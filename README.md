# Aquilo Thermometer: Updated

Estimates the continuous heat needed to keep your force's structures from freezing on Aquilo and other planets that require heating.

Use the heating-tower button in the mod toolbar to show or hide the panel. The close button hides it; click the toolbar button to reopen it. Visibility is also available in Settings → Mod settings → Per player and is remembered independently for each player.

Choose a heating-tower fuel in the panel to see its estimated consumption per minute. Rocket fuel is selected initially. Compatible modded fuels are included, and each player's selection is saved separately. Clearing the selector hides the consumption estimate until another fuel is chosen.

The reading refreshes when opening the panel, changing fuel, changing the viewed surface or force, and when using the refresh button. It does not scan continuously in the background.

The estimate uses entity prototype heating requirements. It does not include initial warm-up energy, heat-network losses or fuel used by production machines.

Requires Factorio 2.1.24 and Space Age. Enable this edition in place of the original; existing saves are supported.
